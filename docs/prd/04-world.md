# PRD — World
> Physics, office tiers, environment story, AI companion, vehicles, clothing

## 12. Physics System

Success should physically affect the scene.

### Physics-Enabled Objects

- Chair
- Papers
- Coffee cup
- Phone
- Desk objects
- Upgrade crates
- Wall fragments
- Confetti
- Coins
- Reward particles

### Small Sale

- Phone vibration
- Small particle burst
- Light sound
- Tiny camera response

### Large Sale

- Chair rolls slightly
- Papers move
- Stronger particle effect
- Lighting pulse
- Cinematic camera movement

### Major Milestone

- Wall cracks
- Wall fragments fall
- Dust appears
- Locked door opens
- New office is revealed

Physics should be satisfying but controlled.

## 21. AI Companion

Optional small robot or AI assistant.

### Purpose

- React to progress
- Celebrate
- Point toward targets
- Show milestone reminders
- Provide light guidance

### Example Behavior

Sale:
- Celebrates

Near Target:
- Points toward progress board

Idle:
- Performs quiet background behavior

Level Up:
- Visually upgrades

The companion must not interrupt constantly.
