## 10. Facial Emotion System

The character must not feel like a static NPC.

### Neutral

- Relaxed eyebrows
- Normal mouth
- Natural blinking
- Calm breathing

### Focused

- Slightly narrowed eyes
- Brows angled inward
- Slight forward lean
- Reduced random motion

### Excited

- Raised eyebrows
- Wider eyes
- Smile
- Faster head movement

### Happy

- Larger smile
- Raised cheeks
- Relaxed brows

### Proud

- Small controlled smile
- Chin slightly raised
- Shoulders back

### Frustrated

- Tight lips
- Brows inward
- Slight head shake

### Surprised

- Wide eyes
- Raised eyebrows
- Slightly open mouth

### Determined

- Focused eyes
- Tight jaw
- Upright posture

### Emotion Blending

Expressions should transition naturally.

Example:

**Neutral → Surprise → Excited → Proud → Neutral**

Avoid hard switching.

## 11. Body-Language System

### Low Activity State

- Slower movement
- More sitting
- Casual posture
- Looking at laptop
- Drinking coffee
- Looking around office

### High Performance State

- Faster walking
- Shoulders back
- Confident idle
- Stronger gestures
- More energetic celebration

### Near Target State

- More focused posture
- Looks toward target screen
- Checks phone
- Leans toward monitor
- Less casual idle behavior
