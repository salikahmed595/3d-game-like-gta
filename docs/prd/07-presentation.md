# PRD — Presentation
> Camera, sound, visual effects, HUD

## 30. Camera System

### Default Camera
Third-person office camera.

### Sale Camera
Quick focus toward character.

### Big Sale Camera
Cinematic orbit.

### Upgrade Camera
Moves toward upgraded object.

### Boss Camera
More dramatic angle.

### Building Unlock
Camera pulls outside the building.

Cinematics should remain short.

## 31. Sound Design

Required:

- Phone ring
- Sale confirmation
- Coin / credit sound
- XP sound
- Level-up sound
- Upgrade sound
- Big-sale sound
- Target-achieved music
- Boss attack
- Boss defeat
- Trophy sound
- Construction
- Car engine
- Confetti

Mute control is mandatory.

## 32. Visual Effects

Possible:

- Confetti
- Particle bursts
- Floating XP
- Money particles
- Light pulses
- Dust
- Wall destruction
- Sparks
- Aura
- Fire streak
- Construction effects

Effect intensity should scale with achievement importance.

## 33. Main HUD

Keep the UI minimal.

Example:

**Top Left:** SALIK BUILDS  
**Top Center:** $18,750 / $20,000  
**Top Right:** LEVEL 17 — CLOSER  
**Bottom:** NEXT UNLOCK: EXECUTIVE OFFICE — $1,250 LEFT

The 3D environment should remain the main interface.
